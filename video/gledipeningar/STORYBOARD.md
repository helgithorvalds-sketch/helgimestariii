# VILD: storyboard

A 156-second, 16:9 (1920x1080, 60 fps) motion-graphics pitch. Takk krónur is used to explain the mechanism. The pitch is **VILD**, the same idea built for restaurant and bar chains, where the credit is called *Vildarpeningar*.

- **Structure:** a short Takk opening (the coin, then 65.000 members), then five chapters, each opened by a card. The camera zooms into an object, the frame floods with the chapter colour (iris), the card appears, and the answer follows. The three idea cards inside chapter 5 work the same way.
- **Tempo:** 110 BPM (a little slower than the first cut). One beat is 0.545 s and one bar is 2.18 s. Every cut, pop and stamp lands on a beat, and each chapter card gets a half-time "breath" in the music.
- **Look:** soft clay 3D with flat 2D infographics on top. Chapters 1–4 (Takk) stay on the lilac studio floor in Takk purple. From the VILD drop in chapter 5 the whole world turns green: fresh mint and lime backgrounds, an emerald night, a dawn-green bedroom, dark green `#0C5C3B` text and light green `#B4FF9B`, with yellow `#FFD24D` as the accent for credit amounts.
- **Brand:** the VILD coin logo (a light green coin with an inner ring and a bold V, with an offset shadow) is used on the 3D coin, the app icon and the notifications. The end card uses VILD's circular-arrow mark and thin, widely spaced wordmark. Both are redrawn as vectors from the supplied artwork.
- **Sound:** an original synthesized score and sound effects, generated from the same timeline. No voiceover.
