# VILD: storyboard

A 139-second, 16:9 (1920x1080, 60 fps) motion-graphics pitch. Takk krónur is used to explain the mechanism. The pitch is **VILD**, the same idea built for restaurant and bar chains, where the credit is called *Vildarpeningar*.

- **Structure:** a short Takk opening (the coin, then 65.000 members), then five chapters, each opened by a card. The camera zooms into an object, the frame floods with the chapter colour (iris), the card appears, and the answer follows. The three idea cards inside chapter 5 work the same way.
- **Tempo:** 110 BPM (a little slower than the first cut). One beat is 0.545 s and one bar is 2.18 s. Every cut, pop and stamp lands on a beat, and each chapter card gets a half-time "breath" in the music.
- **Look:** soft clay 3D on a lilac studio floor, with flat 2D infographics on top. Takk purple runs through chapters 1–4. VILD brings in its own light green `#B4FF9B` with dark green `#0C5C3B`.
- **Brand:** VILD's own mark (a circular arrow around a V) and thin, widely spaced wordmark, redrawn as vectors from the supplied artwork, used flat in 2D and raised on a 3D coin.
- **Sound:** an original synthesized score and sound effects, generated from the same timeline. No voiceover.
