**Through-line object:** *sýrukerin* — the two iron-banded wooden whey vats in the
storehouse. Shown in every block (insert or background), threatened more each block,
used in B6, empty in B7 (the name), one small vat lashed on the ship in B8.

**Compression notes (adaptation):** the uncles' prologue (ch. 1, Grásíða) is held for
episode 4, where the sword returns as a spear. Kolbjörn (the coward who wouldn't fight
Skeggi) is cut; the statues mocked Gísli *and* Kolbjörn — we show Gísli only. The revenge
after the fire (burning Kolbjörn, killing Skeggi's sons, beheading Skeggi) is compressed to
"Þau hefna sín". The twelve others who burned in the fire are not shown.
