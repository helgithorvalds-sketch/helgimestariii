## 1. Brief text (paste as one block)

VELKOMIN HEIM — a 70-second silent short film advertising the SVIF welcome book (@prop_b-klingur), delivered to new homeowners in the Reykjavík area. No dialogue; ambience and music only. Three acts. Act 1, the Strætó: shy Bjarki (@bjarki_1) is reading the SVIF book on a yellow city bus; Sóley (@s-leey_1) sits down opposite him; he falls for her in one slow push into his eyes, she giggles, he hides behind the book; at the stop he does "after you", steps off first and face-plants on the wet pavement; she laughs and pulls him up. Act 2, three fast warm cuts: café, a picnic on the grass, an evening walk on the seafront, the book visible in every frame. Act 3, the house: they buy a small corrugated-iron house, the living room is empty and stays empty as seasons pass, they argue over paint swatches, the doorbell rings, the postman delivers their own SVIF book, they flip through it and agree for the first time; one continuous camera drift through the house as it renovates around the lens; night, sofa, her head on his shoulder, crane up and out of the window; end card SVIF / Velkomin í nýja heimilið þitt / svif.is.

Look: cinematic realism, shot like a Scandinavian bank commercial, quiet and dry. ARRI Alexa 35 image, Zeiss Supreme Prime lenses, 24 fps, 180° shutter, shallow but not gimmicky depth of field, light 35mm film grain, Kodak Vision3 500T feel. Lighting is natural and soft throughout: overcast Icelandic daylight outside, window light inside the bus and café, one hanging bulb and a grey window in the empty house, warm tungsten practicals and TV glow only once the house is finished. Colour: cool, desaturated blue-grey and moss exteriors in Acts 1–2, the same palette indoors with pale skin kept natural; Act 3 warms shot by shot into amber tungsten. Camera movement is slow and deliberate, one move per shot: locked-off, slow push, slow track, one crane; handheld only as a faint breathing micro-movement inside the bus. Performances small and real; no beauty-filter skin, visible pores, matte finish. Aspect 16:9. Every prompt tags Elements by handle and describes only what is in the frame.

## 4. Lighting rules (so every clip matches)
- Exteriors: overcast sky is the key light. Never direct sun, never golden hour, never rim light.
- Bus and café: the window is the key. Faces lit from the window side, the far side falls off naturally.
- Empty house: one grey window plus one hanging bulb (off until shot 19). Nothing else.
- Finished house: floor lamp, TV glow, warm; the window shows blue evening. This contrast is the payoff.
- Skin: matte, natural, pores visible, muted catchlights. Same phrase in every character prompt: "natural skin".

## 5. Sound and edit notes (yours, but the picture assumes them)
- Bus ambience drops under the music on shot 5 and comes back on shot 6.
- Silence for one full second on shot 10b before her laugh.
- Doorbell is the only hard sound effect in Act 3.
- Music: one piece, restrained, warms with the house. Ends on the crane in shot 21, end card silent.
